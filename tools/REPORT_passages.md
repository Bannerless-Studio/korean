## Manual QA (2026-09-25, first build + polish round)

- **Source and build.** The 60 passages are hand-written for this pack in
  polite 해요체. 반말 appears only inside quoted family and friend speech at
  B1 (p0041, p0044, p0049). The authoring sources assemble into
  `tools/passages_src.json`. The assembler sorts questions into reading
  order, cycles each mc key through positions 0-3, and adds every declared
  name to `oop` with a reason starting "name". Two runs give byte-identical
  `pack/passages.json`.
- **Register rule.** Notices, announcements and formal letters use 합쇼체
  (-습니다/-ㅂ니다, -십시오): the six A1/A2 notices p0006, p0015, p0024,
  p0027, p0033, p0036, plus the B1 museum notice p0043 and complaint letter
  p0042. This is the register a
  learner meets on real signs and letters; everything else is 해요체.
- **Builder.** vocab-engine packbuilder plus passage-only Korean hooks in
  `langs/ko.py`: `passage_retag` (with `passage_retag_names`) and
  `passage_lemma_alias`. The word and sentence build never call them.
  `passages.py` has one additive change: `Linker.retag` passes the declared
  names to `passage_retag` when the spec sets `passage_retag_names` (ko only).
  - Names: Hangul has no capitals, so a declared name is made PROPN by the
    hook and split from its particles or copula (민수는, 김민수예요, 지영이는).
    0 name tokens link a pack word.
  - Numeral policy: every part of a numeral compound links its own pack
    numeral, one span per part; no compound links its first digit alone.
    Sino-Korean: 이천 -> 이 + 천, 삼만 -> 삼 + 만, 오십만 -> 오 + 십 + 만,
    십이 층 -> 십 + 이. Native: 열한 -> 열 + 하나, 열네 -> 열 + 넷, 스물여섯
    -> 스물 + 여섯, 예순다섯 -> 예순 + 다섯. The counter after a numeral is the
    counter noun (열네 살 is not 살다). A known word is never split (주민들
    사이도 stays 사이 + -도). Numerals are not counted for coverage.
  - Light verbs: 공부하다 and other pack X하다/X되다 verbs link the verb,
    including 시작하고 (read by the analyser as 시작 + the particle -하고
    "and") and 발견되었거나 (unknown to the analyser). An X하다 verb the pack
    lacks links its noun as one tap (운동해요 -> 운동, 이사해요 -> 이사,
    긴장해서 -> 긴장); audit: 0 noun links where the pack has the verb.
  - Honorifics: -시- forms link the plain verb (타셨어요 -> 타다, 가르치세요 ->
    가르치다). 드세요 -> 먹다 and 드렸어요 -> 주다 come from the build rules;
    the hook adds 주무시다 -> 자다 (unused so far). 교수님 links 교수.
  - Analyser repairs: noun + particle splits that are verb forms (같이 자요,
    못 자는, 고치는, 보지만), whole-eojeol dictionary words the pack lacks
    (사면, 사신, 앞에서), 저 as "that" before a bare noun and as "I" before a
    name, and 자기 before 전 or after 잠 as 자다.
- **Homographs.** The first build's claim that 날 and 안 were checked was
  wrong; QA found both. Now fixed and audited over every span:
  - 날 "day": the analyser read bare 날 as 나 "I" + object particle. The hook
    relinks it to the noun 날 (27 spans, 0 left on 나). 사고가 날 위험 (p0054)
    correctly stays 나다.
  - 안 "inside": the pack has only 안 adv "not", so 가방 안에, 버스 안이에요
    and the other inside uses link it. `tools/gloss_display.json` shows it as
    "not (안 가요); inside (가방 안에)" for all such spans (the display
    applies at the next pack build; words.json is not hand-edited).
  - 알려/알렸/알린 now link 알리다 "to inform" (3 spans), 번 돈 links 벌다.
  - Also checked against the English: 눈 eye/snow, 다리, 차 tea/car, 사과,
    들다/듣다, 살다/사다, 걷다/걸다, 것/거, 한 (one/did), 이/그/저 det vs
    pronoun vs numeral. "이 층" (this floor / second floor) no longer occurs;
    floors read 삼 층 etc., and 일 층 is "first floor" throughout.
  - Sentences the analyser could only read one way were rewritten: 여기는
    "here", bare 산 before 뒤, 오 분 만에, compounds the pack lacks
    (지하철역, 기차표, 빵집, 이삿짐).

  드시다, 상처를 받다).
